Automotrix AI sales assistant

Automotrix, explained

A friendly assistant that greets every customer, finds the right car, books the visit and hands your team a ready-to-close lead. It works from your own records, and your team stays in charge.

Answers in secondsDay or night, in English or Spanish.
Facts from your recordsPrices and cars come from your own database.
Your team stays in chargeA person can step in at any moment.

Part two

How it stays safe and honest

Think of the assistant as a very polite messenger in a building with good doors. Messages are checked on the way in, every answer is checked on the way out, and the important facts are never the messenger's to invent.

Tip: every arrow into the assistant is something it is given. Every arrow out of it passes a check first.

Automotrix is a demonstration dealership in San Antonio, Texas. Text messages and Google Calendar are planned and shown as future skills; everything else on this page is working today.
