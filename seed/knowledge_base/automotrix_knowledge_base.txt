SECTION 4: FINANCING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 GENERAL
- We work with 20+ lenders, including national banks, credit unions,
  captive lenders (the manufacturers' own finance arms), and subprime lenders.
- You may apply online (soft credit pull pre-qualification) or in store.
- Pre-qualification does NOT affect your credit score. A full
  application results in a hard inquiry. Multiple auto inquiries within
  a 14-45 day window are usually treated as one inquiry by credit
  scoring models.
- You are welcome to bring outside financing (your own bank or credit
  union pre-approval). We will honor it, and we may try to beat the rate.
- All rates are subject to lender approval. The chatbot and sales staff
  cannot guarantee approval or a specific rate.

4.2 TYPICAL APR RANGES (ESTIMATES ONLY - NOT AN OFFER)
Rates vary by credit, term, vehicle age, and lender. Approximate
ranges customers commonly see:

  Credit tier            Score     New vehicle APR    Used vehicle APR
  Super prime            781-850   4.5% - 6.5%        6.5% - 8.5%
  Prime                  661-780   6.0% - 8.5%        8.5% - 11.0%
  Near prime             601-660   9.0% - 12.5%       12.5% - 17.0%
  Subprime               501-600   12.5% - 16.0%      17.0% - 21.5%
  Deep subprime          300-500   15.0% - 18.0%+     21.0% - 24.0%+

Promotional manufacturer APR (for example 0.9% - 3.9%) may be available
through our franchise partners on select new models for well-qualified buyers. Promotions change
monthly; ask a sales associate for current offers.

4.3 LOAN TERMS
- Available terms: 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, and 84 months.
- 84-month terms are generally limited to newer vehicles (model year
  within ~3 years) and strong credit.
- Used vehicles older than 10 years or over 125,000 miles may have
  limited financing options or require shorter terms.
- Minimum amount financed: $5,000.

4.4 DOWN PAYMENT
- No money down is possible for well-qualified buyers.
- Recommended: 10% - 20% down to lower payments and avoid being
  "upside down" (owing more than the car is worth).
- Buyers with limited or challenged credit are often asked for $1,000 -
  $3,000 down or 10%+ of the sale price.
- Accepted down payment methods: cashier's check, personal check
  (subject to verification), debit card, credit card (up to $3,000),
  wire transfer, or trade-in equity.

4.5 DOCUMENTS NEEDED FOR FINANCING
Bring the following to speed up approval:
  1. Valid driver's license or state ID
  2. Proof of income:
     - W-2 employees: 2 most recent pay stubs (within last 30 days)
     - Self-employed: last 2 years of tax returns OR 3-6 months of
       bank statements
     - Other income (Social Security, pension, disability): award letter
  3. Proof of residence: utility bill or bank statement from the last
     30 days showing your name and current address
  4. Proof of insurance (or we can help you get a quote)
  5. List of 5-6 personal references (name, phone, relation) - often
     required by subprime lenders
  6. Trade-in title or payoff information (if trading in)
  7. Down payment

4.6 FIRST-TIME BUYERS, NO CREDIT, BAD CREDIT
- First-time buyer programs are available. A co-signer, larger down
  payment, or proof of steady employment (typically 6+ months at the
  same job) improves approval odds.
- Past bankruptcy: many lenders will approve after discharge. Bring
  discharge papers. Chapter 13 buyers need trustee approval.
- Repossession history: financing is possible, usually with a higher
  down payment.
- ITIN financing: available through select lenders for customers
  without a Social Security number. Typical requirements are an ITIN,
  valid passport or consular ID, proof of income, proof of residence,
  and a larger down payment (often 20%+).

4.7 CO-SIGNERS AND CO-BUYERS
- Co-signer: guarantees the loan but is not on the title.
- Co-buyer: on both the loan and the title.
- Both must be present or sign electronically, and provide ID and
  proof of income.

4.8 LEASING (NEW VEHICLES ONLY)
- Terms: 24, 36, or 39 months.
- Mileage options: 10,000, 12,000, or 15,000 miles per year.
- Excess mileage charge: typically $0.15 - $0.25 per mile.
- Lease-end: return the vehicle, buy it for the residual value, or
  trade into a new lease.
- Typically requires a credit score of 680+ for the best advertised
  lease offers.
- Due at signing usually includes first month's payment, acquisition
  fee, tax, title, license, and any down payment (cap cost reduction).

4.9 PAYING CASH
- Cash purchases are welcome.
- Federal law requires us to file IRS Form 8300 for cash payments over
  $10,000 (including cashier's checks/money orders under $10,000 in
  some cases). This is routine and does not affect you if funds are
  legitimate.

4.10 EARLY PAYOFF
- Most of our lenders use simple-interest loans with no prepayment
  penalty. Always confirm with your lender.

SECTION 5: TAXES AND FEES (TEXAS)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Texas motor vehicle sales tax: 6.25% of the sale price, minus the
  value of any trade-in (trade-in tax credit).
  Example: $30,000 car - $10,000 trade = $20,000 taxable x 6.25% =
  $1,250 tax.
- Documentary (doc) fee: $225. This covers preparation of title,
  registration, and sale documents. Charged on every sale.
- Title fee: $28 - $33 (varies by county).
- Registration: approximately $50.75 base plus county fees
  (total usually $65 - $95 for a standard passenger vehicle).
- New Texas residents bringing a vehicle from another state pay a
  $90 new-resident tax instead of sales tax (not applicable to vehicles
  bought here).
- Temporary tags: issued at sale, valid 30 days. Permanent plates are
  mailed or available for pickup within about 2-3 weeks.
- All advertised prices EXCLUDE tax, title, license, and doc fee
  unless stated otherwise.

SECTION 6: TRADE-INS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 WHAT WE ACCEPT
- We accept trade-ins of any year, make, or model, running or not,
  including cars, trucks, SUVs, and motorcycles.
- We will also buy your vehicle outright even if you do not purchase
  from us.
- We accept vehicles with loans (we pay off your lender) and leased
  vehicles (subject to lessor's rules; some lessors restrict third-party
  buyouts).

6.2 HOW THE APPRAISAL WORKS
1. Bring the vehicle in; appraisals take about 20-30 minutes.
2. A used car manager inspects exterior, interior, tires, mechanical
   condition, and takes a test drive.
3. We run a vehicle history report (Carfax/AutoCheck) and check
   current auction and market data (e.g., Manheim, KBB, Black Book).
4. You receive a written offer valid for 7 days or 500 miles,
   whichever comes first, as long as condition does not change.

Online estimate: Use our "Value Your Trade" tool on the website for an
estimate. The final offer requires in-person inspection.

6.3 WHAT AFFECTS YOUR TRADE VALUE
- Mileage and age
- Accident history and title status
- Mechanical issues, warning lights
- Tire and brake condition
- Interior wear, odors (smoke, pets)
- Aftermarket modifications (lifts, tints, wheels) may lower value
- Local demand and season
- Number of keys (a missing key typically reduces offer by $200-$400)

Why trade value is lower than private sale: the dealer must pay for
reconditioning, inspection, holding costs, and warranty risk. In
Texas, trading in also saves sales tax on the trade value, which often
narrows the gap.

6.4 WHAT TO BRING
- Title (if owned outright). If multiple owners are on the title, all
  must sign or be present.
- Loan payoff info: lender name, account number, and a 10-day payoff
  quote if possible.
- Current registration
- Valid ID for every person on the title
- All keys and remotes
- Owner's manual and service records (helpful, not required)

6.5 NEGATIVE EQUITY
- If you owe more than your trade is worth, the difference (negative
  equity) can often be paid in cash or rolled into the new loan,
  subject to lender approval and loan-to-value limits.

6.6 TITLE RESTRICTIONS
- Salvage and rebuilt titles: accepted for outright purchase only,
  at reduced value.
- Flood-damaged vehicles: accepted for wholesale only.
- Out-of-state titles: accepted; may add processing time.
- We cannot accept vehicles with no title, a lien that cannot be
  verified, or a reported theft.

6.7 AFTER THE TRADE
- Remove personal items and toll tags (TxTag, EZ TAG).
- We pay off your loan within 10 business days. Continue making your
  payments until you receive confirmation the loan is paid.
- Cancel your insurance on the traded vehicle only after the deal is
  complete.

SECTION 12: WARRANTIES AND LEMON LAW
---------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW-VEHICLE FACTORY WARRANTY (typical coverage; varies by manufacturer):
- Basic: commonly 3 years / 36,000 miles (some brands 4 yr / 50,000)
- Powertrain: commonly 5 years / 60,000 miles (some brands 10 yr / 100,000)
- Hybrid and EV components: commonly 8 years / 100,000 miles, and federal
  law requires at least 8 years / 100,000 miles on EV traction batteries
- Corrosion perforation: commonly 5 years / unlimited miles
- Included scheduled maintenance: offered by some brands, not all
Coverage follows the vehicle's original in-service date, not the date you
buy it. The remaining term for a specific car is confirmed by VIN.

TEXAS LEMON LAW (summary): New vehicles with a serious defect covered
by warranty that the dealer cannot fix after a reasonable number of
attempts may qualify for repurchase or replacement. Complaints are
filed with the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV). Contact our
customer relations manager first.

SECTION 15: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Can I get approved with bad credit?
A: Many customers with challenged credit get approved. Approval
   depends on income, job stability, down payment, and the lender.
   Pre-qualify online with no impact to your credit.

Q: Do I need a Social Security number to buy a car?
A: No. We work with lenders that accept ITIN, and cash buyers do not
   need an SSN.

Q: Will you buy my car if I don't buy one from you?
A: Yes. We buy vehicles outright.

Q: I still owe money on my car. Can I trade it in?
A: Yes. We pay off the loan. If you owe more than it's worth, the
   difference can be paid or possibly rolled into your new loan.

Q: Can I return the car?
A: Texas has no cooling-off period. We offer a 3-day / 150-mile
   exchange on used vehicles.

Q: Are you open Sunday?
A: Sales and service are closed Sundays. Online chat is available
   24/7.

Q: Is the price negotiable?
A: Used vehicle prices are market-based and competitive. Talk to a
   sales associate about your specific situation, trade-in, and
   available incentives.

Q: How long is my trade offer good for?
A: 7 days or 500 miles, if condition doesn't change.

Q: What credit score do I need?
A: There is no strict minimum. Scores above 660 usually get better
   rates; lower scores may need a larger down payment.

Q: Can I use my own bank?
A: Yes. Bring your pre-approval letter.

Q: Do you speak Spanish?
A: Sí. Tenemos personal bilingüe en ventas, finanzas y servicio.
